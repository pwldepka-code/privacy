# Account & Data Deletion — PUBDOM Cards

_Last updated: 28 May 2026_

This page explains how to request deletion of your PUBDOM Cards
account and the personal data we hold about you.

## What gets deleted

When you request account deletion, we permanently remove:

- Your Google account identifier (`sub`), email, display name, and
  avatar from our `users` and `players` collections
- Your full game progress: card collection, coin balance, opened
  packs, missions, achievements, leaderboard entries, profile likes
- Your cross-device inventory snapshot
- All references to your account in the Hall of Fame leaderboard
- Any pending reward / claim records tied to your account

The deletion is **irreversible** — there is no recovery, and
reinstalling the app and signing in again with the same Google
account will start a fresh profile from zero.

## What is retained (and why)

For legal and accounting reasons we retain the following for the
periods stated:

- **In-app purchase transaction records** — retained for at least
  7 years to comply with financial-record-keeping obligations
  (tax, fraud disputes, refunds). These records are stored in
  pseudonymised form (only the in-app user id, product purchased,
  amount, and timestamp — no name, email, or payment instrument).
- **Aggregate, non-identifying analytics** (e.g. "X total packs
  opened across all players this week") — retained indefinitely
  because they contain no personal information.
- **Server access logs / crash logs** — retained up to 90 days.

If you also want your Google Play purchase history deleted, that is
controlled by Google, not by us — see
[Google Pay account closure](https://support.google.com/googleplay).

## How to request deletion

Send an email to **pubdomcards.support@gmail.com** _(replace with your
real email)_ with:

1. The subject line: **"Account deletion request"**
2. The body must include: the Google email address you sign in with
3. _(Optional)_ Your in-app display name, to help us double-check the
   right account

Send the email **from the same Google address you use to sign in**.
This is how we verify you own the account.

## What happens next

- We acknowledge your request within **3 business days**.
- We complete the deletion within **30 days** of acknowledging.
- After completion we send you a confirmation email and the account
  is permanently gone.

We never charge a fee for handling a deletion request.

## Questions

If you have any questions about deletion, retention, or what data we
hold, email **pubdomcards.support@gmail.com**. Our full privacy
practices are described at
<https://pwldepka-code.github.io/privacy/>.
